## 2. Where we want to be

- Framework matrix per layer (see `test-system.md`): `bun test` for unit, integration and smoke; Playwright for E2E.
- Single coverage floor of 80% line and 80% branch, enforced by the reporter.
- Layer-split CI (unit, then integration, then smoke) within the 15 minute budget.
- Zero tests in `rebuild` or `delete (redundant)` buckets.

## 3. The gap

- **Coverage is invisible.** CI emits no coverage artifact, so the line gate reads `not reporting` and nothing enforces the 80% floor.
- **Layers are not separated in CI.** One `bun test` step runs unit, integration and smoke together, so there is no fail-fast and no per-layer timing.
- **Four source files have no test**, three of them in the runtime. `runtime/boot.ts` (1,015 lines, critical) and `runtime/crypto.ts` (security) matter most. These keep 4 of 13 features out of the covered count (8 of 13 covered).
- **No PR template** with a verification-output checklist.
- Chat routes and a few scripts look uncovered by name but are tested through sibling suites or are environment probes; see Open risks.

## 6. Open risks

- **Feature coverage is a static, name-based estimate** (8 of 13), not a measured figure. It under-counts files tested indirectly (chat routes via `messages|threads|tokens.smoke.test.ts`, `chat/src/main.ts`, `runtime/entrypoint-main.ts`) and may over-count files a test merely imports. The first lcov report (T-001) is the real gap list; re-run this pipeline after it lands.
- **Files left without a task on purpose:** `chat/src/main.ts`, `chat/src/routes/*` (smoke-covered by sibling suites), `runtime/entrypoint-main.ts` (thin wrapper over the tested `entrypoint.ts`), `admin/src/chat-credential-config.ts`, `admin/scripts/backfill-member-id.ts` and the environment probes `scripts/verify-*.ts`, `scripts/generate-chat-spec.ts`. A human should confirm that is acceptable.
- **Test-body depth is unverified.** Large units (`admin-ui.ts`, `admin-ui-pages.ts`, `agents-api.ts`, `slack.ts`, `setup.ts`, `claude.ts`, `agent-provisioner.ts`) have nearby tests, but no coverage data exists yet to show depth.
- **`platform-routes.ts` is tested at unit only** while the inventory prescribes smoke; confirm the wire contract is covered by `admin-spec` or route smoke tests.
- **T-004 is a GitHub Settings change** and needs a human. Until it lands, the split jobs are advisory.
- **Tier promotion signals** (re-classify a feature upward when any fires): feature flag removed, traffic ramp to 10% of users or more, milestone marked general availability, or an engineer explicitly flags it. Re-run `/test-inventory` from scratch after a new service boundary or top-level user journey.
- **`deploy_model` is undeclared.** Declare `## Deploy model` in `CLAUDE.md` to enable canary planning.
